Projects I chose to do myself

Outside class, I pick a public dataset and a question I can't answer yet.

Excel · 2026

Private equity LBO model

Taught myself leveraged buyout modelling (BIWS). I read Watches of Switzerland's annual reports and built a 3-statement LBO model for a possible £1.6 billion deal. My recommendation: invest, because the IRR stays above 20% even if the company misses management's forecasts.

Python, Excel · 2026

Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption

Combined IMF PortWatch ship-tracking data with FRED oil prices. Ship traffic fell from about 95 to 5 a day, 13 times worse than the COVID-19 low, and the WTI–Brent spread blew out from $3.96 to $23.83.

Python, Excel · 2026

Queensland cross-domain forecasting

Linked 12 public datasets (QGSO, ABS, Queensland Treasury, BOM) in a knowledge graph to find early warning signs that single-sector models miss. For example, construction wages predict hospitality insolvency 6–9 months ahead.

Python, Power BI · S2 2026

Co-operative sector analysis for BCCM

Matched Australia's Top 100 co-operatives to ABS SEIFA data. Housing has the most active co-ops of any sector (232) but none in the Top 100.
